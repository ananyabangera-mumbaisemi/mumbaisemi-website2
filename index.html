01. About & Mission

Indigenous semiconductor IP from IIT Bombay for a global market

Spun out of the aiCAS Lab at IIT Bombay and incubated at SINE, MumbaiSemi is a fabless semiconductor company designing high-performance RF, Analog, Mixed-signal, and Digital ICs for advanced communications, AI/ML hardware, autonomous vehicles, and low-power IoT.

Our core strength lies in developing innovative circuits to address the challenges in chip design, from multi-band RF front-ends operating in extreme environments to high-speed PHYs for AI/ML hardware and data centers.

Our Mission

We research and innovate to produce disruptive solutions for futuristic challenges to develop high-quality semiconductor chips in India for national and global needs.

03. Flagship Silicon

DhruvaPro

DhruvaPro is India’s first fully integrated, indigenous reconfigurable RFIC for multi-constellation navigation across NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, and GLONASS, making it a universal solution.

The RFIC is silicon-verified and developed as a production-level IC with ESD protection, on-chip testing, and circuits engineered for excellent performance with low power consumption using patented ideas.

  • Multi-constellation GNSS: NavIC, GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, GLONASS
  • Silicon-verified and developed as a production-level IC
  • On-chip ESD protection & built-in self-test (BIST)
  • Packaged in QFN-32 for evaluation in real environments
  • Circuits capable of operating at extreme temperatures
